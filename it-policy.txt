Acme Corp IT Security Policy

Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
Employees must enable two-factor authentication on all company accounts.
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Report lost or stolen devices to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.
Public Wi-Fi may only be used together with the company VPN.
Personal USB drives are not allowed on company computers.
