Acme Corp Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days.
Sick leave is separate and allows 12 days per year with a doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days.
Employees must apply for annual leave at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
The office is closed on public holidays announced by the government.
